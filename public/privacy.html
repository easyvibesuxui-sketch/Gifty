Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2025. We keep this simple and honest.

 Short version: We don't store your searches, don't sell your data, and don't show you ads. We earn through affiliate commissions when you buy products we recommend.

What information we collect

Giftly is designed to collect as little data as possible. When you use the AI Gift Finder, your search query is sent to Google Gemini's API to generate recommendations. We do not log or store these queries on our servers.

  • We do not require you to create an account
  • We do not store your search history
  • We do not collect your name or email unless you contact us
  • We do collect standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) which are automatically deleted after 30 days

Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies to make the site function. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies for retargeting. See our Cookie Policy for the full list.

Affiliate links & third parties

When you click a product link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer (e.g. Amazon Associates). This comes at no cost to you. We disclose all affiliate relationships clearly on the site and in our Affiliate Disclosure.

Your purchase is processed entirely by the retailer — we never see your payment information.

Data security

All connections to Giftly are encrypted via HTTPS. We host on Cloudflare's network, which provides enterprise-grade security and DDoS protection. We do not store sensitive personal data, so there is minimal risk from any breach on our side.

Contact us

For any privacy questions or data requests, contact us at privacy@giftly.ai or use our contact form.
